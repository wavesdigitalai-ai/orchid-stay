Around the stay

A practical guide to Avinashi Road.

You came for the hospital, the airport, or the trade fair. Here is what is close, what is easy, and where to eat while you are here.

KMCH · across the road Airport · 1.2 km CODISSIA · 2.7 km

01 — For hospital visits

KMCH is across the road.

If you are accompanying a patient, the last thing you need is a long commute. Orchid Stay sits directly opposite KMCH Hospital, so you can be at the gate in a minute and back in your room shortly after.

We often host families here for hospital visits and offer simple weekly rates. Tell us how long you are staying and whether you would prefer a quieter room away from the road — we will do what we can.

  • KMCH main gate1 min walk
  • Pharmacies & diagnostics2–4 min walk
  • Restaurants & tea shops2 min walk
  • Weekly attendant rateAsk reception
Staying a while? Message us with your dates before you arrive and we will confirm the current weekly rate.

02 — For flights

Land, and be here in four minutes.

Coimbatore International Airport is about 1.2 km away. That makes Orchid Stay an easy choice for early departures, late arrivals, and short layovers.

Tell us your flight details when you book and we will help arrange a pickup. If you are leaving early, let reception know the night before so we can have everything ready.

  • Coimbatore International Airport1.2 km · 4 min
  • Airport pickupOn request
  • Early check-inSubject to availability
  • ReceptionOpen 24×7

03 — For work

Close to the business day.

CODISSIA Trade Fair Complex is 2.7 km away — about seven minutes by car. The Peelamedu and Avinashi Road business belt, PSG institutions, and Covai Tech Park are all within a short drive too.

If you are here for an exhibition or a series of meetings, tell us your schedule and we will help you plan an easy start.

  • CODISSIA Trade Fair Complex2.7 km · 7 min
  • Peelamedu3 km · 8 min
  • Covai Tech Park5 km · 12 min
  • Coimbatore Junction11 km · 25 min

04 — For eating well

Breakfast is two minutes away.

Avinashi Road does not leave you hungry. Within a short walk you will find South Indian breakfast, biryani, chaat, coffee, and a few reliable local messes.

Ask reception for their current favourites — they know which places are quick, which are good for families, and which stay open late.

  • Annapoorna · Avinashi Road2 min walk
  • Shree Anandhaas3 min walk
  • Ambur Star Biryani2 min walk
  • 10 Downing Street6 min walk

Coimbatore is a good base for a short break. These are a few places worth the drive if your schedule allows.

Getting around

Simple logistics, sorted.

A few things worth knowing before you arrive. Anything unclear? Reception is awake around the clock.

From the airport

1.2 km — about 4 minutes by car. A prepaid taxi or app cab is easy to find. Ask us about a pickup when you book.

From the railway station

Coimbatore Junction is 11 km away (25–30 minutes). Podanur Junction is further out; allow extra time in traffic.

Getting to KMCH

The hospital is directly across Avinashi Road. Use the signal-controlled crossing and allow a minute or two.

Parking

On-site parking is limited. Tell us before you arrive and we will try to hold a spot. Street parking is also available nearby.

What to carry

A valid government photo ID for every adult. PAN cards are not accepted as ID proof. Couples are welcome.

Need something?

Call or WhatsApp reception at any hour. We will help with directions, food suggestions, or an early wake-up call.
