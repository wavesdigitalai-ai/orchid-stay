Before you arrive

Good to know.

A few practical details that make check-in quick and easy. Anything else? Message us on WhatsApp.

Check-in & check-out

Check-in from 12:00 PM, check-out by 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out is often possible on request, subject to availability.

ID & guests

Couples are welcome. Every adult guest must carry a valid government photo ID. PAN cards are not accepted as proof of address.

Payment

No payment is taken online. Payment is made at check-in. Your room is confirmed once reception replies on WhatsApp.

Flexible plans

Plans change. Message us as early as you can and we will do our best to move your dates or adjust your stay.

Parking

On-site parking is limited to a small number of cars. Let us know in advance if you are driving and we will try to reserve a spot.

Pets & smoking

We are unable to host pets. All rooms are non-smoking for the comfort of every guest.
